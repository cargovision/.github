## 🚢 What is Cargovision?

**Cargovision is an AI-powered container inspection platform for ports and logistics ecosystems.**
It replaces slow, manual gate inspections with automated, camera- and image-based analysis —
reading container identities, detecting structural damage, and generating instant digital
reports. The result: cargo moves faster, with higher accuracy and full accountability, and
**without opening or physically checking every container**.

## ⚠️ The Problem

Indonesia's logistics sector is growing rapidly, but **manual inspection remains the biggest
bottleneck at ports**. As cargo volumes rise, undigitized physical inspection triggers queues
and costly delays — affecting every stakeholder, from port operators to the national economy.

## 💡 How Cargovision Solves It

Cargovision standardizes inspection through data, turning a manual checkpoint into an automated
intelligence layer that runs *without disrupting existing port workflows*.

| Step | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| **1. Capture** | Live camera feeds and uploaded images are ingested at the gate and inspection bays. |
| **2. Identify** | Container IDs, owner codes, and size-type codes are read automatically — no manual logging. |
| **3. Inspect** | Structural damage and anomalies are detected and classified in real time, with severity, location, and confidence. |
| **4. Report** | A structured digital report with annotated visual evidence is generated instantly — ready for compliance, audit, and handover. |

**Core capabilities**

- 🔎 **Live OCR Identification** — automatic container ID, owner & size-code reading at every gate entry.
- 🛡️ **AI Damage Detection** — real-time detection and classification of structural defects and anomalies.
- 📄 **Real-Time Reporting** — instant digital records, chain of custody, and one-click PDF reports.
- 📊 **Dashboard Insight** — a unified, real-time view of inspection status, performance, and compliance.
- 🤖 **AI Assistant** — query inspection data and generate insights through natural language.

> **A note on technology:** Cargovision is built on modern **AI and computer-vision** technology,
> delivered as a mobile and web platform. To protect our work, we intentionally keep our models,
> system architecture, and infrastructure details private.

## 🌟 Vision &amp; Mission

> **Vision** — To become the pioneer and primary standard in Indonesia's integrated logistics
> inspection ecosystem, actively driving operational efficiency, data transparency, and national
> maritime competitiveness.

**Mission**

1. Deliver high-accuracy visual inspection and data-analytics innovation that eliminates operational bottlenecks across port areas.
2. Build a consistent, tamper-resistant digital documentation infrastructure that supports compliance with customs regulations and national standards.
3. Create an integrated ecosystem that delivers tangible added value for all supply-chain stakeholders through throughput optimization.

## 📈 Business Model

- **SaaS Subscription** — pay-per-container or monthly usage.
- **Enterprise License** — annual licensing for institutions.
- **Add-ons** — system integration, advanced analytics, and custom AI training.
